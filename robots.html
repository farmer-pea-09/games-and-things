Robot Lab

$45 Robot Ideas

Reuse your Nano. Cardboard is free. Each build stays at or under forty-five dollars. No mic. No LiPo. No official Arduino tax.

What you already own

Arduino Nano, one SG90, one button, USB cable, jumper wires, cardboard, hot glue.

The $45 is extra parts for that one robot — leftover packs become the next robot.

  • Do buy: clone Nano if you need a spare (~$7), SG90 packs, 5 V power bank, Dollar Tree junk.
  • Do not buy: official Nano, LiPo + charger, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, metal chassis kits.

Switch Motor

Toggle on, DC motor runs. Toggle off, it stops. MOSFET does the work so the Nano pin does not fry.

~$16 extra sketch ready

Buy (~$16)

  • Slide / toggle switch — $2
  • MOSFET module — $7
  • Small DC motor — $6
  • Diode if you skip the module — $1

Reuse Nano, USB / 5 V pack, jumper wires. Motor pack and Nano share GND. Do not feed the motor from the Nano 5V pin.

Pins

Switch D2 → GND. MOSFET SIG D3. LED D13 on while it spins.

Sketch: switch-motor/switch-motor.ino

Temp Birdbath

12 V heat under the bowl. Probe in the water. Thaws ice, never cooks birds. Optional foam bird sips when it is liquid.

~$44 extra sketch ready

Buy (~$44)

  • Waterproof DS18B20 probe — $7
  • MOSFET module — $7
  • 12 V 2 A wall adapter — $9
  • 12 V heat pad / PTC + pie tin under the dish — $12
  • Plant saucer if you need a bath — $4
  • Dry box + silicone — $4
  • 2 A fuse — $1

Reuse Nano, USB charger, servo, button. Whole-kit ceiling ~$44. Skip the saucer if you already have a plastic bath.

The rule

Heat is DRY under the bowl. 12 V only. GFCI on the wall. No mains in the water. No galvanized tub. Water stays ~39–46 °F, lockout at 55 °F.

Pins

Probe D4 (4.7k to 5V) · heater MOSFET D3 · optional pump MOSFET D5 · bird servo D9 · button D2.

Libraries: OneWire + DallasTemperature. Sketch: temp-birdbath/temp-birdbath.ino
